# Berkeley-MLAI-Cert-Mod-5_1-Coupons-Assignment
Required Assignment 5.1: Will the Customer Accept the Coupon?

This project consists of analyzing data of acceptance of coupons given to various drivers with variouse demographics and consumer spending patterns.  I specifically focused on bar coupons and foud that there is a high correlation and probability of coupon acceptance amongst younger consumers and for those who frequent bars during the month.

I also looked at coffee consumers and proved out my hypothesis that people going in the same direction of the store are more likely to accept the coupon than those who are going in the opposite direction.
